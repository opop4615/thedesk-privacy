The Desk: Trading Roguelike — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-01 · Developer Sungki Bae (sungkisoft) · Contact opop4615@naver.com · 한국어

1. In short

The Desk collects no personal data. There is no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchase. The game never connects to the internet, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

2. What stays on your device

The game saves the following in its own app storage on your device. None of it is sent anywhere, and the developer cannot see it.

WhatDetails
Run in progressEra, trader, map, deck, desk, account, the trade you are in
RecordsBest grade per era and trader, unlocked difficulty levels
NotebookNotebook entries you have opened and their levels
SettingsLanguage, sound, screen effects, chart settings, guides already seen

Deleting the app deletes all of it. On Android, device backup is turned off for this app, so the data is not copied to your Google account.

3. What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It requests no device permissions.
  • It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It shares nothing with third parties, because it has nothing to share.

4. Market data and news in the game

The prices, economic indicators and news summaries shown in the game are historical records bundled with the app. They are not fetched from the internet and they are not based on anything about you.

5. Not investment advice

The Desk is a game made for entertainment and education. It is not investment advice and not a solicitation to trade. Results in the game say nothing about results in real markets.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If a later version starts collecting any data (for example an online leaderboard), this policy and the store disclosures will be updated before that version ships. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: opop4615@naver.com
